# Work / Study To-Do List

## Plan
- [ ] Choose the three most important things to complete today
- [ ] Break larger tasks into smaller steps
- [ ] Gather the notes, materials, and tools I need

## Focus
- [ ] Work on the highest-priority task
- [ ] Take a short break, then continue with the next task
- [ ] Check messages and email at a planned time
- [ ] Review or practice what I learned

## Wrap up
- [ ] Finish or note the next step for any task in progress
- [ ] Record what I completed
- [ ] Choose a first task for the next work or study session
